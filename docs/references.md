# References and provenance

Primary publisher, author, project and ESO sources below were checked on **2026-09-12**. A citation identifies the origin of an equation or design motivation; it does not imply validation of this simulator by its authors. No observational data, fitted instrument parameters or proprietary information are used.

## Instrument context

1. **ESO.** [NIRPS: Near Infra-Red Planet Searcher](https://www.eso.org/sci/facilities/develop/instruments/NIRPS.html). The site's *Baseline Specifications* table lists HAM resolving power 100,000 and sampling of three pixels per FWHM; its *Front End* description identifies the 3.6 m telescope. These three values motivate the demonstration. The page contains historical design descriptions: its different sections should not be combined into a claim about current operational performance. Seeing, obscuration, WFE, jitter, transmission, detector noise and source flux in this repository are assumptions, not NIRPS measurements.
2. **Marconi, A., et al. (2024).** [ANDES, the high resolution spectrograph for the ELT: science goals, project overview and future developments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14601). arXiv:2407.14601; [DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2407.14601](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14601). Motivation for AO-fed near-infrared high-resolution spectroscopy. This repository does not implement ANDES optics, its ELT pupil or its AO control system.
3. **ESO.** [Testbench for photonic chips](https://www.eso.org/sci/activities/fellowships-and-studentships/engstudentfellow/photonic_chips.html). Motivation for a future laboratory programme using injection optics, cameras, line lamps and fibre-fed lasers.

## Physical model

4. **Fried, D. L. (1966).** [Optical Resolution Through a Randomly Inhomogeneous Medium for Very Long and Very Short Exposures](https://opg.optica.org/josa/abstract.cfm?uri=josa-56-10-1372). *Journal of the Optical Society of America*, **56**, 1372–1379. [DOI: 10.1364/JOSA.56.001372](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSA.56.001372). Fried coherence length and the connection between phase statistics and image quality.
5. **Tokovinin, A. (2002).** [From Differential Image Motion to Seeing](https://www.ctio.noirlab.edu/~atokovin/papers/pasp2002.pdf). *Publications of the Astronomical Society of the Pacific*, **114**, 1156–1166. [DOI: 10.1086/342683](https://doi.org/10.1086/342683). Equation 5 gives the conventional Kolmogorov seeing relation, $\epsilon_0=0.98\lambda/r_0$, and its wavelength convention. The author-hosted paper also explains why finite outer scale modifies actual long-exposure image width.
6. **Fétick, R. J. L., Neichel, B., Mugnier, L. M., Montmerle-Bonnefois, A., and Fusco, T. (2018).** [Turbulent and adaptive optics corrected point-spread functions as convolutive orders of the phase power spectral density](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/481/4/5210/5127368). *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*, **481**, 5210–5215. [DOI: 10.1093/mnras/sty2595](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2595). Equation 17 explicitly gives the von Kármán phase PSD with coefficient 0.023 and spatial frequency in cycles per metre.
7. **Goodman, J. W. (2017).** [Introduction to Fourier Optics, fourth edition](https://www.macmillanlearning.com/ed/uk/product/Introduction-to-Fourier-Optics-4th-edition/p/1319119166). W. H. Freeman/Macmillan Learning. ISBN 9781319119164. Standard derivation of scalar Fresnel/Fraunhofer propagation and the Fourier-transform relation between pupil amplitude and focal-plane field; cited as an authoritative textbook rather than a new experimental result.
8. **Mahajan, V. N. (1983).** [Strehl ratio for primary aberrations in terms of their aberration variance](https://opg.optica.org/josa/abstract.cfm?uri=josa-73-6-860). *Journal of the Optical Society of America*, **73**, 860–861. [DOI: 10.1364/JOSA.73.000860](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSA.73.000860). Basis for using $\exp(-\sigma_\phi^2)$ as the extended Maréchal cross-check and for restricting conclusions to small aberrations.
9. **Shaklan, S., and Roddier, F. (1988).** [Coupling starlight into single-mode fiber optics](https://opg.optica.org/ao/abstract.cfm?uri=ao-27-11-2334). *Applied Optics*, **27**, 2334–2338. [DOI: 10.1364/AO.27.002334](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.27.002334). Complex-field overlap and the effects of seeing, wavelength and image displacement on single-mode injection. Their physical fibre-mode result is not imposed as the maximum of the Gaussian approximation used here.
10. **Birks, T. A., Gris-Sánchez, I., Yerolatsitis, S., Leon-Saval, S. G., and Thomson, R. R. (2015).** [The photonic lantern](https://opg.optica.org/aop/abstract.cfm?uri=aop-7-2-107). *Advances in Optics and Photonics*, **7**, 107–167. [DOI: 10.1364/AOP.7.000107](https://doi.org/10.1364/AOP.7.000107). Authoritative review of adiabatic multimode-to-single-mode transitions and mode-capacity constraints. The ideal modal projection and assumed transmission in this repository are a deliberately reduced model, not a digitisation of a measured lantern.
11. **Leon-Saval, S. G., Argyros, A., and Bland-Hawthorn, J. (2010).** [Photonic lanterns: a study of light propagation in multimode to single-mode converters](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.008430). *Optics Express*, **18**, 8430–8439. DOI: 10.1364/OE.18.008430. Original modal-propagation analysis supporting the mode-capacity condition; complementary to the Birks et al. review.
12. **Bouchy, F., Pepe, F., and Queloz, D. (2001).** [Fundamental photon noise limit to radial velocity measurements](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2001/29/aa1316.pdf). *Astronomy & Astrophysics*, **374**, 733–739. [DOI: 10.1051/0004-6361:20010730](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20010730). Spectral-gradient information and the photon-noise RV limit. The simulator generalises the weighting to include its assumed detector variance.

## Future public-data work

The operational collection describes median resolving powers of 82,000 (HA) and 75,000 (HE); these differ from the historical 100,000 design value chosen for this conceptual demonstration. [Release announcement](https://archive.eso.org/cms/eso-archive-news/release-of-nirps-pipeline-reduced-spectra.html), [release description](https://www.eso.org/rm/api/v1/public/releaseDescriptions/233). These are discovery and schema references only: no member of the collection was downloaded or analysed for this project. The [public-data procedure](public_data.md) states the explicit import boundary and required provenance for future work.
